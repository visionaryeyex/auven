# AUVEN Agent Core

The mark builds an **A** from two geometric arms. A connected diamond at its center stands for the agent identity and the decisions that pass through the gateway.

- `assets/auven-x-avatar.png` — 1024 × 1024 square avatar for X, with safe margins for circular cropping.
- `assets/auven-x-avatar.svg` — vector source for the avatar.
- `dist/logo-mark.svg` and `dist/logo-mark-white.svg` — symbols used by the site.
- `dist/logo-horizontal.svg` — horizontal wordmark.
- `dist/favicon.svg` — browser icon.

Colors are taken directly from the site: purple `#7057E8`, dark `#24202F`, and white `#FFFFFF`. Keep the center diamond and its negative space intact when resizing the mark.
